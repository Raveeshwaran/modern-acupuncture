Explore your intake   ↗
What is included in the online intake?

The digital intake covers optional personal details, reasons for visiting, medical history, medications, allergies, emotions, sleep, symptom checklists, optional reproductive health, a seven-day menstrual chart, pain assessment and a front/back pain map. The original question set is retained; your meridian reference image is included.

Do I need an account to get started?

The site opens with sign-in. You may close it to read the public information. To save an intake or request a time, create an account with your email address and a password of at least 12 characters.

Can I pause and come back to my intake?

Yes. Choose Save draft before leaving. You can sign in again and continue from the saved information. The standalone demo stores sample records on this device; the server edition stores them on the server.

Is a time request a confirmed appointment?

No. This demonstration records a preferred date and time. It is not connected to a real clinic calendar and does not send email confirmations. In the server edition, your assigned doctor can confirm requests. The standalone demo uses simulated appointments.

Is this ready for real medical information?

No. This is a working software demonstration. A real clinic must arrange secure hosting, operational safeguards, privacy and legal review, verified practitioner access, and appropriate clinical oversight before collecting patient information.

What if I need urgent medical help?

This website is not monitored for urgent concerns. Do not use the intake or appointment requests for emergencies. Contact the appropriate emergency medical service in your area.

Where is the practitioner-only section?

The tongue and pulse assessment from page six is restricted to practitioner-role accounts. Only the server owner can grant that role; new accounts cannot grant it to themselves.
